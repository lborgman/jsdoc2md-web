Parses common JSDoc tags (@param, @returns, @example, @typedef, @property, @throws, @deprecated, @extends)
        client-side. "Exports only" keeps functions, classes, and class methods reachable via export,
        export default, export { }, or CommonJS exports.x /
        module.exports — @typedef blocks are always kept since they're type-only. Not a full
        jsdoc-parse-compatible AST — good enough for most real-world files, but exotic syntax may not resolve.
